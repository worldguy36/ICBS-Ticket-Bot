## Section 1: About You

### Question 2
- **Title:** What is your Discord User ID?
- **Description:** To find your User ID: enable Developer Mode in Discord (Settings → Advanced → Developer Mode), then right-click your own username → Copy ID. It will be a 17-19 digit number.

- **Required:** Yes
- **Response validation:** Number, greater than or equal to 10000000000000000 (17 digits)

### Question 3
- **Title:** How old are you?
- **Description:** We ask for maturity reasons — there's no upper or lower limit, but honesty matters more than the number. Support staff handle sensitive situations and need to be reliable.
- **Type:** Short answer
- **Required:** Yes
- **Response validation:** Number, between 13 and 99

- **Required:** Yes
- **Options:**
  - UTC-12 to UTC-8 (Pacific / Alaska)
  - UTC-7 to UTC-5 (Mountain / Central / Eastern US)
  - UTC-4 to UTC-1 (Atlantic / Brazil)
  - UTC+0 (UK / Portugal / Iceland)
  - UTC+1 (Central Europe / West Africa)
  - UTC+2 (Eastern Europe / Egypt / South Africa)
  - UTC+3 to UTC+5 (Russia / Middle East / Pakistan)
  - UTC+5:30 to UTC+7 (India / Southeast Asia)
  - UTC+8 to UTC+10 (China / Japan / Australia)
  - UTC+11 to UTC+14 (Pacific Islands / NZ)

## After the Form is Created

1. **Set up response destination:** Link the form to a Google Sheets spreadsheet so you can review applications in a structured way.
2. **Set up email notifications:** Form → Settings → Notifications → "Get email notifications for new responses".
3. **Create an application review checklist** in the linked Sheet with columns for: Applicant Discord tag, Application date, Initial screen (pass/fail), Interview scheduled (yes/no), Final decision, Notes.
4. **Send a confirmation DM** to each applicant within 48 hours of submission — even if just to say "we got your application, we'll be in touch".
5. **Decision timeline:** Aim to give every applicant a final yes/no within 7 days. Ghosting applicants is the #1 way to lose goodwill in the community.

## Evaluation Rubric (for reviewers)

When reviewing applications, score each section 1–5:

| Criterion | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Did they answer the question actually asked? Vague or off-topic answers are a red flag. |
| **Maturity** | Do the scenario answers show emotional regulation, empathy, and judgment? |
| **Availability** | Do their hours fill a coverage gap? A 5-hours/week applicant in an empty time zone is more valuable than a 20-hours/week applicant in a peak time. |
| **Honesty** | Did they admit to past warnings? Did they say "I don't know" to anything? Honesty > perfection. |
| **Specificity** | Generic "I'd be polite and help them" answers score low. Specific ("I'd ask for screenshots, steps to reproduce, and the exact error message") score high. |
| **Hunger** | Does the "why do you want this" answer feel genuine or copy-pasted? |

**Auto-reject triggers:**
- Lies about past warnings/bans (we will check)
- Hostile or dismissive tone toward hypothetical users
- "I want power" or "I want to ban people" as motivation
- Application clearly copy-pasted from another server's form
- Discord username doesn't match a real server member

**Interview prompts** (for shortlisted applicants):
1. "Walk me through how you'd handle a ticket where the user is clearly lying about a bug to get free stuff."
2. "Show me your DMs are open — I'm going to send you a test message right now."
3. "What's a ticket you'd escalate to a senior staff member vs handle yourself?"
4. "How would you feel about closing 30 tickets in a row that are all 'I forgot my password'?"
